# FinRCA-Bench data audit (M0)

Status: engineering audit of upstream data at pinned revision `76c93ec32536e29826e2ab9e68b58e0351410bc7` (checkout `data/upstream/FinRCA-AI-Bench`, retrieved 2026-11-01; provenance and licence in `DATA_SOURCES.md`). It is not expert financial adjudication. Paths below are relative to the checkout unless they start with `/` or are a Reconcile file. "Unverified" marks anything I did not check.

## 1. Scope and method

Question: can FinRCA support an honest evaluation of an investigator for F06 (invoice paid twice), F12 (ERP payment missing from bank) and F14 (bank/ERP amount mismatch), plus legitimate look-alikes, before anything is built (PLAN.md sections 2 and 6/M0)?

Method:

1. Read the documentation, schema, taxonomy, generator code for the three families and hard negatives, the splitter, the question generator, the evaluators and the frozen Rules/SQL specification (paths in each section).
2. Read the operational tables `data/benchmark/full/*.csv` with throwaway Python (standard library, `python3 -I` or scratchpad scripts kept outside the repository; nothing from upstream was executed).
3. Read the per-split label files for **train and validation only** (`data/benchmark/train/*`, `data/benchmark/validation/*`). During the audit, test and challenge files were hashed and line-counted only; the later baseline reproduction read more (section 11 is the current record).
4. Traced records to conclusions with predicates that encode the label definitions (taxonomy lines 70-81, 148-159, 174-185). A passing predicate shows the label is consistent with the operational records. It is not independent adjudication of whether the cause stated by the label is true.
5. Hand-read full record cards for about 20 cases (including F06 RCA_000501 and RCA_000578, F12 RCA_001103 and RCA_001156, F14 RCA_001302 and RCA_001304, and one case of each legitimate variant) before trusting the predicates.

Cutoff used for all age calculations: 2026-06-30 (`config.yaml` line 6; `docs/frozen_rules_sql_baseline_spec_v1.0.md` line 19). Business days are Monday to Friday with no holiday calendar (same spec). Clearing limits ACH 3, wire 1, check 10, virtual card 2 business days (spec line 745; `config.yaml` lines 38-40 for ACH, wire and check; the card limit appears only in the spec).

## 2. Taxonomy and family mapping

Source: `docs/failure_taxonomy.md`; class names in `data/benchmark/dataset_statistics.md` lines 42-56.

| PLAN.md family | Upstream class | Taxonomy lines | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suspected duplicate payment | `F06_INVOICE_PAID_TWICE` | 70-81 | Mapping confirmed. Mutation: insert a second full-value payment, allocation, journal and bank debit for a fully paid invoice (line 74). Single scenario variant in the data (`separate_payment_runs`, all 76 train+validation cases). Root-cause category `DUPLICATE_DISBURSEMENT`. |
| Missing settlement | `F12_ERP_PAYMENT_MISSING_FROM_BANK` | 148-159 | Mapping confirmed. Mutation: delete the matched bank row (line 152). Three sub-causes under one class (`PAYMENT_RELEASE_QUEUE_TIMEOUT`, `PAYMENT_TRANSMISSION_FAILURE`, `PROCESSOR_BATCH_REJECTION`), distinguishable only by audit event name (section 4, L3). |
| Amount discrepancy | `F14_BANK_ERP_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` | 174-185 | Mapping confirmed. Mutation: lower the bank amount by a fixed fee of 15, 20, 25 or 35 units (`src/failures/f14_bank_erp_amount.py` line 26) with no fee journal (line 179). Single variant `unrecorded_net_fee`, category `UNRECORDED_SETTLEMENT_FEE`. Bank and ERP currencies are equal in every training and validation F14 case (checked, section 7). |

Required distinctions from PLAN.md section 1:

- Duplicate versus instalments or reversals: represented. Hard negatives `legitimate_split_payment` and `legitimate_batch_payment` (allocations equal the invoice, taxonomy line 80) and `legitimate_payment_reversal` (bank credit plus reversal journal). **Repeated source ingestion of a payment is not represented**: there are zero duplicate primary keys (`data/benchmark/dataset_quality_report.json` `duplicate_ids`, all 0) and the only duplicate-ingestion class, F01, concerns invoices.
- Missing settlement versus ordinary clearing delay: represented by `legitimate_pending_settlement` (taxonomy line 158), `legitimate_weekend_clearing` and `legitimate_check_clearing`.
- Unsupported difference versus documented fee: represented by `legitimate_net_fee_accounting` (taxonomy line 184, fixed at 25 units, `src/failures/hard_negatives.py` line 326), `legitimate_accounted_bank_fee` and `legitimate_fx_settlement`.

Legitimate cases: label `failure_type == "NO_FAILURE"` in `rca_ground_truth.jsonl` (`docs/data_dictionary.md` line 139; `README.md` lines 93-95). There are 16 variants (`scenario_variant`, listed in section 5). Ten are payment related and are the relevant look-alikes for the three families: pending settlement, weekend clearing, check clearing, due-date payment, split payment, batch payment, payment reversal, net-fee accounting, accounted bank fee, FX settlement. The other six (exact PO match, PO tolerance, split cost centre, credit memo, invoice cancellation, pre-invoice terms change) are AP-side and are out of scope for the three families. **Legitimate pending payments** are `legitimate_pending_settlement`; **fees** are `legitimate_net_fee_accounting` (fee netted from the bank amount, source-linked fee journal) and `legitimate_accounted_bank_fee` (separate bank-fee row carrying the ERP payment reference, with a balanced fee journal).

## 3. Field classification

Three zones per PLAN.md section 2. Operational means the field could exist in an AP ledger or bank export before an analyst reaches a conclusion.

### 3.1 Observable operational input (enumerated allowlist)

Files: `data/benchmark/full/*.csv`, the 14 tables of `docs/schema.md` lines 7-22. This one snapshot contains every case of every split (all records, including test and challenge entities); the split is a label-side property only.

Rule: a column is an input only if it appears in the tables below. Any column, table or event type not listed is dropped. This is an allowlist, not a denylist (PLAN.md section 2); a column added upstream later is excluded until audited. Columns I did not examine for class correlation are dropped, not kept.

Column actions:

- **keep**: passed unchanged.
- **remap**: replaced by an opaque token. One keyed random mapping per identifier namespace, with the key discarded after the run, so the same value maps to the same token in every table and column where it appears (genuine matches survive) and the token carries no order, length, prefix or format of the original. Namespaces: `vendor` (`vendors.vendor_id`, every `vendor_id` column), `po`, `po_line`, `invoice`, `invoice_line`, `payment`, `bank_txn`, `journal`, `journal_line`, `approval`, `audit_event`, `statement`, `change`, `payment_ref` (`payments.reference_number` and `bank_transactions.payment_reference` share it, so payment-to-bank matching by reference works), and `bank_acct_token` (vendor and counterparty tokens). A polymorphic ID column (`gl_entries.source_transaction_id`, `audit_log.entity_id`) is mapped with the namespace of the entity it points to.
- **drop**: removed.
- Row order: every table is re-sorted by its opaque key (or shuffled with a fixed seed) before use. In `full/` the rows are in insertion order: the 200 inserted payments are rows 6001-6200 of `payments.csv`, the 147 inserted invoices rows 8001-8147 of `invoices.csv`, and every ID column is ascending (checked on all of these tables). Row position is a leak (L2, L12).

`audit_log` is dropped whole, not filtered to a type list. Reason, from checks on `full/` (train and validation labels for the per-family rows):

- `payment_created` (6000 events): present for 6000 of 6000 organic payments and 0 of the 200 inserted payments (reference above 6042). Presence alone identifies every inserted F05/F06 payment (L4). The same event is also the only one whose date differs from `payment_date` for pending payments (47 of 47; 33 of 33 pending cases in train and validation, L5). Evidence payments carry it in F12 71 of 71, F14 76 of 76, and in every payment-related legitimate variant, so it does not separate those classes; it separates only the inserted payments.
- Every other kept type has no signal for F06, F12 and F14 but adds nothing needed: `approval_completed` (8000 events, invoices) and `received` (7622) / `received_recurring` (378) exist for 8000 of 8000 organic invoices and 0 of the 147 inserted invoices (F01 duplicates and credit memos); the `received` timestamp date equals `invoices.received_date` in 7622 of 7622; `created` (5000, purchase orders) and `master_data_changed` (40, vendors) duplicate `po_date` and `vendor_change_log`. In train and validation evidence, the invoices cited in F06 cases carry `received`/`received_recurring` in 76 of 76 and `approval_completed` in 76 of 76, as do the legitimate invoice variants; no kept type separates F06, F12 or F14 from the legitimate look-alikes.
- Simplest change that removes the signal: drop the table. Nothing in the F06, F12 or F14 contract needs it.

Other retained columns checked for a systematic difference between the 200 inserted and 6000 organic payments (`full/`): `created_at` hour is 08 for 6000 of 6000 and 200 of 200, and `created_at` date equals `payment_date` for all of both; weekend `payment_date` 1639 of 6000 (27.3%) against 61 of 200 (30.5%); inserted payments are all `completed`/`settled` and organic ones are 5906/5806 plus pending 47, reversed 47 and failed 100 (the non-completed states belong to legitimate and F11 cases, not to F06/F12/F14); all 6000 and 200 have GL lines sourced to them; the method and currency mixes are the same kinds (inserted: ACH 135, check 34, wire 27, virtual_card 4). `payment_allocations`: 100 of 200 inserted payments have allocation rows against 6000 of 6000 organic (the other 100 are F05 payments without an invoice, a genuine observable of that class, not of F06). Bank rows for inserted payments are all `posted` with the same type mix and posting lags (3, 5, 4 days) as organic ones. Residual, not removed: the 147 inserted invoices differ from the 8000 organic ones on kept columns: `approval_events` rows 0 of 147 against 8000 of 8000, `status` `received` (100) or `credit_applied` (47) against `paid`/`approved`/`canceled`, and `po_id` empty in 83 of 147. These are F01 and credit-memo records, not F06/F12/F14 inputs (the invoice in every F06 case is organic), but a request whose primary entity is an invoice can be classified by them. Handle in the cohort rules (exclude or declare), not by the transform. Bank rows with status `returned` (100) and payments with `failed` settlement (100) are F11 markers, outside the three families, flagged.

## 4. Leakage findings

Each item was checked against the data; counts are for train plus validation labels unless stated.

**L1. Case IDs encode the family.** Case numbers are issued sequentially per injector (`src/failures/pipeline.py` lines 25-30). Train ranges: F06 501-600, F12 1103-1199, F14 1302-1399, NO_FAILURE 1501-2250 (validation ranges are consistent; each family occupies a 100-wide block). The ID appears in four of the five question styles (`src/ground_truth/question_generator.py` lines 40-43). Test and challenge are presumed to follow the same blocks (unverified; not read). Do not give the investigator `case_id`; use opaque request IDs.

**L2. Injected records have tail-range IDs.** IDs come from per-prefix counters (`src/utils/ids.py` lines 16-20) and organic rows are generated first. Payments: organic `PAY_0420001`-`PAY_0426000`; the 200 payments above `PAY_0426000` are exactly the inserted F05 and F06 payments (every one of the 147 in train+validation labels belongs to F05 or F06). So any payment ID above 426000 identifies F06/F05 without reading a single field. Journals: the 400 payment journal lines at or above `JE_0434001` are consistent with those same 200 payments (two lines each; not individually matched); the tail also holds 220 bank-fee, 94 reversal, 94 FX-settlement, 94 cancellation and 189 credit-memo lines, all hard-negative or failure artefacts. Invoices: 147 invoices above `INV_0428000` (F01 duplicates such as `INV_0428005`; other members not mapped, unverified). Bank transactions: injected rows (for example `BT_0426164`, `BT_0426366`) sit above the organic range; boundary not computed. **Deleted rows leave gaps**: 147 bank transaction IDs are missing from `BT_0420001`-`BT_0426410`, against 145 payments with no bank row (99 completed/settled plus 46 submitted/pending). The exact mapping of gaps to payments was not checked (unverified), but the gaps point at F12 and pending cases.

**L3. Anomaly-named audit events (class leakage).** Counts in `data/benchmark/full/audit_log.csv`:

| `event_type` | Events | Class |
|---|---:|---|
| `net_settlement_received` | 100 | one per F14 case |
| `release_queue_timeout` / `payment_file_not_transmitted` / `processor_batch_rejected` | 33 / 34 / 33 | one per F12 case; sub-cause is the event name only (identical `field`, `old_value`, `new_value`) |
| `processor_return_received` | 100 | F11 |
| `workflow_override` | 100 | F08 |
| `manual_payment_created` | 100 | F05 |
| `bank_portal_manual_transfer` | 100 | F13 |
| `auto_match_applied` | 100 | F15 |
| `interface_queue_released` | 100 | F10 |
| `vendor_selected` | 100 | F04 |
| `fx_conversion_applied` / `invoice_canceled` | 47 / 47 | legitimate FX / cancellation |

In train+validation every labelled event maps to one class (checked via `affected_entities` of type `audit_event`). Presence of the event decides the class; none occurs in a legitimate case except the two legitimate-only types in the last row. Upstream's own Rules/SQL freeze excludes these (spec section 12 "anomaly-specific audit event types"), but the Direct-LLM packet serialises the whole `audit_log` and the leakage audit marks it PASS (`docs/direct_llm_case_packet_leakage_audit_v1.0.md` table, `audit_log` row). Published Direct-LLM results therefore had access to class-naming events.

**L4. Missing `payment_created` event marks inserted payments.** Every organic payment has a `payment_created` event; none of the 200 inserted payments do (76 F06 and 71 F05 in labels, 53 others unlabelled here). Counts on `full/`: 6000 of 6000 organic payments and 0 of 200 inserted payments have the event. The artefact disappears because section 3.1 drops `audit_log` whole; it would remain if any `payment_created` row were kept.

**L5. Pending payments have inconsistent timestamps.** `legitimate_pending_settlement` mutates `payment_date`, `created_at`, `payment_status` and `settlement_status` and removes the bank row, but not the audit event (`src/failures/hard_negatives.py` lines 284-300). In the data 47 payments have `payment_created` on a different date from `payment_date`; the 33 in train+validation labels are all pending cases. All 47 pending payments are dated 2026-06-29. Dropping `audit_log` (section 3.1) also removes this signal; `payments.created_at` was mutated together with `payment_date`, so it is consistent (6000 of 6000 organic and 200 of 200 inserted payments have `created_at` on the `payment_date`).

**L6. Ledger types and memos exist only for hard negatives.** GL `transaction_type` values `fx_settlement`, `payment_reversal`, `credit_memo`, `invoice_cancellation` occur only in legitimate cases, as does the memo "Documented net settlement fee" (94 lines = 47 cases). The inverse also holds: no failure case has these journals. Memos ("Documented payment reversal", "Documented FX settlement clearing") read like labels. Journal IDs for all of them are in the `JE_0434xxx` tail (L2).

**L7. Fee amount.** Legitimate net-fee cases always differ by exactly 25 (33 of 33 across train and validation). F14 failures differ by 15, 20, 25 or 35: across train and validation 35 in 25 cases, 15 in 18, 20 in 17, 25 in 16 (76 cases). Legitimate separately accounted bank fees are 15, 35 and other values, equal to F14 deltas. Amount alone does not separate the classes; the source-linked fee journal does.

**L8. Pending age is far from the F12 boundary.** All 47 pending payments are 1 business day old; the smallest F12 margin over the clearing limit is 3 business days (71 labelled cases). The distinction is clean in this snapshot; it does not exercise the boundary and relies on one global cutoff.

**L9. Question wording and entity type.** Detection questions are templated by entity type only (`Is there a reconciliation issue associated with <type> <ID>?`); `gl_journal` appears only for F10 and `bank_transaction` almost only for F13. Within the three families the type separates F06 (`invoice`) from F12/F14 (`payment`) but not from the 284 legitimate invoice-primary cases. The other four styles do not differ by class apart from the embedded case ID.

**L10. Label metadata.** `difficulty`, `reasoning_hops`, `severity` and `scenario_variant` are constant within F06 (medium, 3 hops), F12 (hard, 4 hops) and F14 (medium, 3 hops); all NO_FAILURE cases have severity `none`. Never expose.

**L11. Published baselines are not observable-input baselines.** The classical ML run lists a `primary_entity_type` feature among its strongest (`docs/classical_ml_baseline_evaluation_v1.0.md` section 18) and and its report states it "emits no source-record evidence" (section 1); the Direct-LLM packet includes the full `audit_log` and the case ID route (L3). Published scores (ML exact accuracy 0.9544, Rules/SQL 0.8497 on 439 test cases) are diagnostic, not a counterpart to an observable-input comparison.

**L12. Reference and number fields carry generator order and class, so remapping keys alone is not enough.** Checked in `upstream src/` at the pinned revision and in `data/benchmark/full/`:

- `src/generators/payment_generator.py` line 92: `reference = f"PMTREF-{ctx.ids.counters['PAY'] + (ctx.config['seed'] % 997):010d}"`. `src/failures/f06_double_payment.py` line 40 and `src/failures/f05_payment_without_invoice.py` line 34 build the inserted payments' references the same way. The seed is 42 (`resolved_config.yaml` line 41), so the reference is the payment counter plus 42. In the data, `reference_number` minus the payment counter (payment ID number minus 420000, for example 426101 gives 6101) is exactly 42 for all 6000 organic and all 200 inserted payments; RCA_000501: 6143 - (426101 - 420000) = 42. Organic references run 43-6042; the 200 inserted payments have 6043-6242, so every reference above `PMTREF-0000006042` is an inserted (F05 or F06) payment (200 of 200; 0 organic). RCA_000501 (train): inserted `PAY_0426101` carries `PMTREF-0000006143`, its original `PAY_0423323` carries `PMTREF-0000003365`.
- Training-label check (evidence payments with a reference above 6042): F06 58 of 58 train and 18 of 18 validation cases; F12 0 of 55 and 0 of 16; F14 0 of 58 and 0 of 18; NO_FAILURE 0 of 387 and 0 of 139. A single threshold on the reference separates F06 from F12, F14 and NO_FAILURE on this evidence.
- `src/generators/bank_generator.py` line 53 and 77 copy the value into `bank_transactions.payment_reference`. All 200 inserted payments have a bank row (200 bank rows reference an inserted payment), so the same threshold works on the bank side without the payment table.
- `bank_transactions.bank_reference` (`bank_generator.py` lines 51 and 75; `f13_bank_missing_erp.py` line 42; `hard_negatives.py` line 530): `BANKREF-` equals bank counter plus 42 (6053 rows, offset exactly 42), and the prefixes `BANKFEE-` (63), `MANUAL-` (100, F13) and `REVERSAL-` (47, legitimate reversal) name the row kind. `payment_reference` for F13 rows is `BANKPORTAL-` plus the bank counter (100 rows, F13 only).
- `invoices.invoice_number` (`invoice_generator.py` line 34): `SYN-<vendor suffix>-<sequence>`, and the sequence equals the invoice counter for all 8000 organic invoices (offset 0). The 147 invoices above `INV_0428000` all fail that format: 100 are dash-stripped copies (`SYN2479004672`, F01) and 47 are `CM-...` credit memos (legitimate). In labels, the F01 evidence always includes such an invoice (46 of 46 train, 27 of 27 validation); so do 19 of 387 train and 16 of 139 validation NO_FAILURE cases (credit memos).
- Row order: in `full/` every table is written in insertion order and every ID column is ascending. The inserted payments are rows 6001-6200 of `payments.csv` and the inserted invoices rows 8001-8147 of `invoices.csv`. A consumer that keeps file order keeps the leak even after every ID is remapped.
- `vendors.bank_account_token`, `payments.bank_account_id` and `bank_transactions.counterparty_token` embed the vendor index or a change counter (`vendor_generator.py` line 92; 12 of 500 vendors hold a token index above 500, that is, a changed token); 100 payments and 100 bank rows carry `BA_TKN_SUPERSEDED_` (F11). The allowlist in section 3.1 drops `invoice_number` and `bank_reference`, remaps `reference_number`/`payment_reference` with one shared opaque mapping, remaps every ID, and re-sorts rows. Not yet tested on a built dataset. Unchecked: whether free text or the `audit_log` timestamps of retained events still order the rows (L4, L5).

## 6. Temporal assumptions

- Cutoff: one global date, 2026-06-30, not stored in any table (spec line 1045). Latest `payment_date` and `allocation_date` is 2026-06-29; latest `invoice_date` 2026-06-10.
- Event time versus availability time: not separated. Per-table timestamps are `payments.payment_date` and `created_at` (equal date, always 08:00), `bank_transactions.transaction_date` (initiation) and `posted_date` (bank posting, a usable availability proxy for bank rows), `gl_entries.posting_date`, `audit_log.timestamp`, `approval_events.event_timestamp`. There is no ingestion or `known_at` column, and status columns (`payment_status`, `settlement_status`, `invoices.status`, `bank_transactions.status`) have no update time. The upstream spec states this limitation (spec lines 19 and 1051).
- Consequence: a historical replay at any cutoff earlier than 2026-06-30 would show the final status values and rows that did not exist at that date (for example an F12 payment appears `settled` immediately after payment). Only the single-snapshot assumption is honest: investigate as of 2026-06-30 with all rows treated as available. Do not claim historically faithful replay (PLAN.md section 2).
- Post-cutoff data is present: 2 bank rows have `posted_date` after the cutoff (latest 2026-07-02, both check clearing), 10 GL lines post 2026-07-01, 5 `interface_queue_released` audit events are timestamped up to 2026-07-01, and 168 invoices are due after the cutoff (normal). A cutoff filter would exclude these.
- The injected audit event for F12 is stamped 23:30 on the payment date, and the F14 event is stamped at 18:00 on the bank posting date (F12: all 100 events checked; F14: three sampled events).

## 7. FX and multi-currency

- Currencies in the three families are mixed, not USD only. Training plus validation primary-entity currency: F06 USD 62 of 76 (rest EUR, CAD, JPY, GBP), F12 USD 51 of 71, F14 USD 61 of 76. JPY amounts have no decimals.
- Bank and ERP currencies are identical in all 76 F14 cases in train and validation (61 USD, 6 EUR, 4 CAD, 3 GBP, 2 JPY). So the failure class contains no cross-currency amount comparison.
- Cross-currency bank versus ERP amounts occur only in `legitimate_fx_settlement` (47 cases overall: 21 train, 9 validation). The conversion rate exists only as `audit_log` `fx_conversion_applied` `new_value` (for example `0.74`, `1.08`); the matching `fx_settlement` journal posts a placeholder 1.00 and does not carry the converted amount. There is no rate table, so the conversion cannot be recomputed from ledger data (confirmed: ERP 14294.97 CAD, bank 10578.28 USD, event 0.74, journal 1.00).
- PLAN.md section 4 requires abstention on unsupported FX in v0.1. Consequence: the 30 labelled FX cases (train+validation) can only be abstained, flagged out of scope, or (if misread as F14) counted as false alarms. They cannot be successes for the primary metric (abstentions do not count). Decide whether the release cohort excludes them (section 12, question 4).

## 8. Evidence-contract gradability

What the evaluators define (all from the label files, `evidence_ids` set and `evidence_required` table list):

- `src/rules_sql/evaluation.py` lines 296-351: precision and recall of identifier tokens against `evidence_ids`; "correct and complete" requires the correct class, the frozen internal contract and `expected <= returned`; counted for anomaly decisions only (the `ANOMALY` guards at lines 311, 312 and 319).

Findings (train+validation; 0 of 1608 cases have empty `evidence_ids` or `evidence_required`):

| Class | `evidence_ids` shape | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| F06 | invoice, two payments, one bank row (4 IDs, every case) | 11 of 76 cases involve three payments (instalments plus the inserted one); the contract lists two, omitting a payment that is part of the overpayment sum. Allocation rows are not cited by ID though `payment_allocations` is a required table |
| F12 | payment plus the anomaly-named audit event (2 IDs, every case) | Absence of a bank row and the payment journal (tables `bank_transactions`, `gl_entries` are required) have no ID. The only citable second record is the class-naming event (L3) |
| F14 | payment, bank row, anomaly-named audit event (3 IDs) | The decisive fact (no fee journal) is an absence with no ID; the event again names the class |
| Legitimate, payment related | 1 to 5 IDs, never empty; pending payment lists only the payment; weekend/check list payment and bank row; net fee lists payment, bank row and fee journal; FX lists a `fx_conversion_applied` event; reversal lists payment, debit, credit and journal | Non-empty, so completeness is definable, but for pending the age and the absence of a bank row are not citable. A system could pass completeness by citing one payment ID without showing legitimacy |

Blockers for the PLAN.md primary metric ("correct supported diagnosis and required supporting evidence"):

1. If anomaly-named audit events are excluded from inputs (as they must be, L3), every F12 and F14 `evidence_ids` list (71 and 76 labelled cases) contains an ID the investigator can never see (that is, an event ID; see below). Every F12 and F14 evidence set contains an excluded `EVT_` event (71 of 71 and 76 of 76 cases), so the upstream evidence sets cannot be fully satisfied from allowed inputs. Their payment and bank IDs stay observable: F12 71 of 142 evidence IDs are non-event (the payment), F14 152 of 228. The same holds for the 30 FX (90 of 120 IDs observable) and 31 cancellation (62 of 93) legitimate cases. Pending cases (33, train and validation) have no event in the set (evidence is the payment ID only), so they stay gradable by ID but need an absence/age contract to be graded meaningfully. Either the allowlist keeps the leaking events or the contract must be rederived.
2. Absence evidence (F12, F14, pending) has no record-level contract at all.

Therefore the primary metric is gradable as published only for F06 and for legitimate cases whose IDs are all observable (split, batch, reversal, weekend, check, due date, net fee, accounted bank fee). For F12, F14 and pending it needs a new, explicitly declared and validated contract (for example payment ID, the complete set of searched-and-empty bank rows described by an auditable count, and the fee-journal absence). That derived contract would be our label, not upstream's, and must be fixed before any holdout use (open question 3).

## 10. Ambiguous or unobservable labels

1. **Root causes are narrative, not observable.** F06 "a later payment run did not recognise the prior settlement", F12 sub-causes, F14 "processor netted a fee not configured or posted" are not recoverable from ledger and bank tables. What is observable: F06 cumulative effective allocations exceed the invoice total with two or more settled payments and matching bank debits and no reversal; F12 a completed/settled payment with no non-fee bank row beyond the clearing limit; F14 a unique same-currency bank debit below the payment with no GL journal sourced to the bank transaction. Score the observable condition and treat the sub-cause as unobservable (the sub-cause is available only through anomaly-named audit events, L3).
2. **F06 versus legitimate patterns.** The first leg is a batch payment (RCA_000578) or instalments (11 of 76 cases) in some cases; arithmetic on allocations is the only reliable discriminator, and three-payment cases omit a payment from `evidence_ids` (section 8). Prepayment, on-account credit or intentional overpayment cannot be represented (the Rules/SQL spec F06 section 9 flags this as a limitation).
3. **F06 repeated ingestion** cannot be distinguished from a duplicate committed payment because the data has no ingestion-duplicate examples. A system that deduplicates by amount and date would remove the inserted second payment of RCA_000501 (same amount, two days later) and miss the failure, which is the opposite of the PLAN.md rule to preserve distinct payments.
4. **F12 and pending** depend on one global cutoff and business-day arithmetic without a holiday calendar; pending payments are exactly one business day old (L8). Pending payments were manufactured by relabelling an old payment and removing its bank row (L5); their story is internally inconsistent in timestamps.
5. **F12 with fee rows** (RCA_001156) and legitimate split-payment legs with attached accounted fee rows (RCA_001583, RCA_001711, RCA_001855) show that bank rows sharing a payment reference can be fees, so reference counting is ambiguous.
6. **Shared records with conflicting labels.** 25 non-vendor records in train+validation labels are evidence or affected entities in more than one case: 16 are shared between a failure case (F09, F10, F11, F15) and a NO_FAILURE case, 8 between two legitimate cases, 1 between two F06 cases (`PAY_0425420`, RCA_000519 and RCA_000555). Mixed failure/no-failure sharing contradicts a one-label-per-case assumption for those records.
7. **FX legitimacy** is not verifiable from ledger data (section 7).
8. **Pending evidence** is a single payment ID (section 8), so legitimacy cannot be shown with record citations.
9. **Data oddities, not labels.** Payment dates fall on weekends (for example RCA_000501's inserted payment on a Sunday, ACH); some invoices have `due_date` earlier than `received_date` (RCA_000501); the quality report records `post_injection_invariant_validation.passed = false` with 615 issues (INVOICE_OVERPAYMENT 147, PAID_INVOICE_BALANCE 200, PAYMENT_ALLOCATION_TOTAL 100, PAYMENT_BANK_ORDER 78, APPROVAL_PAYMENT_ORDER 90) while `quality_gate_passed = true`, described as intended findings (`dataset_quality_report.json`). Not investigated further.
10. **Not examined**: approval events, PO/quantity families (F01-F05, F07-F11, F13, F15) beyond the leak counts above; whether `bank_statements` balance (upstream reports they do).

## 13. Verdict: is an honest experiment feasible?

Conditionally feasible; not as the PLAN.md primary metric is currently written.

What works:

- The three families map cleanly to F06, F12, F14; each has well-matched hard negatives (split, batch, reversal, pending, weekend, check, net fee, accounted fee).
- The operational tables are enough to reproduce each observable condition deterministically with Decimal arithmetic (population check, section 9).
- Splits are grouped by vendor with zero overlap; train and validation provide 171 plus 52 three-family cases and 235 plus 80 payment-related legitimate cases for development.

Blockers (all must be resolved or declared before M1 builds on this data):

1. **Class leakage in the snapshot.** Anomaly-named audit events (L3), tail-range and gap-bearing IDs (L2), missing `payment_created` events (L4), inconsistent pending timestamps (L5), hard-negative-only ledger types and memos (L6) and family-encoding case IDs (L1). An allowlist plus opaque IDs and references (L12) can neutralise most of it but each fix must be tested, and the published baselines were not run under it (L11).
2. **Evidence contract not usable for F12, F14 and pending.** With leaking events removed, every F12 and F14 evidence set (71 of 71, 76 of 76 in train and validation) loses its event ID and cannot be satisfied in full; the payment and bank IDs remain observable. Pending cases keep their payment ID but need an absence/age contract. Absence evidence has no contract (section 8). Needs a new, declared and validated contract.
3. **Small, skewed samples.** Under 30 holdout cases per family (test plus challenge), 12 to 48 vendor groups per family in train, vendor concentration up to 13 of 171 cases; test is already exposed through published results.
4. **Label contamination.** Records shared between failure and no-failure cases (25 records, 3 legitimate split-payment cases contradicted) and the F12 fee-row ambiguity.
5. **Scope gaps.** No repeated-ingestion duplicates, a single scenario variant each for F06 and F14, FX legitimate cases that PLAN.md says to abstain on, and no per-case availability timestamps.

If blocker 1 or 2 cannot be fixed the correct M0 outcome is to stop and narrow the claim (for example, an F06-only study plus F12/F14 detection without evidence-completeness) rather than build around FinRCA (PLAN.md M0 gate).
