# Decision records

Short, tracked summaries of the decisions listed in `PLAN.md` section 12 ("Write short decision records for: ..."), as required by `AGENTS.md` section 3. They were written at the start of M3 and cover M0 to M2.

The full working log, `DECISIONS.local.md`, is deliberately untracked and is not part of the repository. These records summarise it. Entries such as "D017" in the Evidence sections are log identifiers, kept as provenance only; the claims rest on the tracked files named next to them. Each record states its status and what would change it.

Numbers quoted here are development results on the synthetic FinRCA benchmark (train and validation splits). They are not sealed-test results, and no practitioner input has been collected yet (`docs/practitioner-findings.md`).
